Method, filters and data limitations

  • Financials use ROE and growth because ROCE, OPM and D/E are not comparable for lenders. Review ROA, NIM, GNPA/NNPA, CASA, CAR, VNB and combined ratio separately.
  • Cyclicals and real estate are shown for relative context; trailing returns can be structurally misleading at different points in the cycle.
  • Rows link to the consolidated company page on screener.in. The screen does not scrape sector KPIs, news, management quality, valuations beyond P/E, or forward estimates.
